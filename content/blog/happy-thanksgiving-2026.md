Today is Thanksgiving in the United States. I’m stepping away from the keyboard for a bit—and if you’re reading this on a rare quiet minute between dishes and cousins, I hope you get a real breath too.

I’m grateful for the teachers and IT folks who still answer tickets when half the district is on break. I’m grateful for the founders and engineers who ship fixes instead of slide decks. And I’m grateful for everyone who’s clicked through this site, tried a browser sim, or sent a note after a post. Building in public only works because someone on the other side cares enough to read.

No product updates today. No launch checklist. Just: **Happy Thanksgiving.** However you’re spending it—around a table, on a video call, or finally alone with a book—thank you for being here.

We’ll be back to the usual deep dives soon. For now, go enjoy the pie.
