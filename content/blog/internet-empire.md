**Internet Empire** is a browser tycoon that asks one question: can you still matter when the calendar hits **December 2030**?

You start in **1995** with **$5,000**, one employee, zero visitors, and a dream that the internet might be a business. Each **month** you queue actions — build a site, buy a domain, hire, ship products, ride macro events from the dot-com bubble through mobile and into the **AI era** — then press **End month**.

The hook I'm proudest of: **the UI changes with history**. Early game chrome feels like **Windows 95**; the dot-com years go glossy; Web 2.0 gets card UI; by the 2020s you're looking at a dense **enterprise dashboard** (with an optional simplified accessible mode). A **Three.js side stage** shows a tiny office that grows with your infra tier.
