Over the past few weeks, I’ve spent more focused time building games than in my previous year of casual dabbling. Paper designs, broken prototypes, discarding ideas that only worked in my head—the usual cycle. It finally pushed me to stop treating “studio” as a someday concept and put a real name on the work.

That’s **[Novux Games](https://novux.dev)**.

Novux is where commercial titles will live. [House of Strategy](/work/hos/), [Startup Simulator](/work/startup-sim/), and the other browser tools under [Work](/work/) remain personal side projects. Same builder, different containers:

- **Studio updates & releases:** [novux.dev](https://novux.dev)
- **Interactive side projects & experiments:** [austinbuhltech.com](https://austinbuhltech.com)

## Dynasty: College Basketball

Our first title is **Dynasty: College Basketball**, heading to **Android**.

Text on screen, recruiting classes, rebuild years, watching a program climb or fall apart over multiple seasons. No cutscenes required. Mobile still doesn’t have enough of that kind of depth without card pulls and live-service padding wrapped around it.

You run a college basketball program: roster, recruiting, seasons that carry over. Data-driven and text-first, built for phones. If you’d rather live in the stats and recruiting board than watch canned dunk animations, you’re the audience.

**Dynasty** is in active development. No release date in this post. Dev logs and milestones go on [novux.dev](https://novux.dev) as stuff actually locks in.

ClassQuip is still the day job. Novux is nights-and-weekends with a logo on it.

I’ll keep writing here about WebRTC, edtech, and whatever else I’m tinkering with. Big Novux news may show up here too; day-to-day studio chatter belongs on the studio site.
