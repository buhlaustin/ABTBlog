I added a new game to the site: **[Executive Email Roulette](/work/eer/)**.

You’re the interim CEO. Every day, five emails show up—HR meltdowns, activist investors, something the founder saw on Twitter, the usual. Each message gives you **two reply buttons**. Pick one, watch four meters move (stock, board approval, employee morale, cash), and try not to get ousted. If any meter hits 0% or 100%, you’re done.

It’s deliberately small. I wanted something you could finish a “day” of in a few minutes, in the same spirit as *[Reigns](https://www.devolverdigital.com/games/reigns)*—swipe left or right, live with the consequences—except the tone is exhausted corporate instead of medieval.

Some of the emails are hand-written. When the narrated mode is on, the game can also generate a fresh inbox for that day so the crises don’t repeat as quickly. Same idea as the host lines in [House of Strategy](/work/hos/): flavor and variety, not the core rules. The meters and the two-choice structure are the game; the rest is seasoning.

If you want the long season, alliances, and eviction nights, play HOS. If you want inbox roulette and a board that’s always one bad reply away from calling security, try EER.
